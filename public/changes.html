Reading this page

Parties that entered or left the lists when the snapshot was last rebuilt, and every rebuild before it. This is the rescreening trigger: a party added here may already be in your book, and a party removed here may be one whose funds you are still holding.

  • How often. The snapshot is rebuilt every night at 23:00 UTC from all six authorities. If a source failed, a recovery run retries it at 11:00 UTC. The comparison is between two consecutive builds, not two dates you choose. If a build was skipped, its changes fold into the next one.
  • Matching across builds. A party is matched by the authority's own entity id. An authority that reissues a party under a new id shows as one removal plus one addition.
  • Source status. The line under the title shows, for each authority, when its data was retrieved. If a source was down, its records are carried forward from the last good build. A carried-forward authority shows no changes for that night: nothing was compared, so nothing changed. That is different from it having published nothing new.
  • Bulk swings. A bulk swing is one authority adding or removing 500 or more parties in a single rebuild. That is an authority returning to or leaving the snapshot, or reissuing its ids, not a day of designations. The history hides those rows unless you ask for them.
  • Expired BIS denials are not removals. A BIS Denied Persons order whose end date has passed is still published, so it does not appear here as a removal. Its record card and search result say the order has expired.
  • Changes within a record are not listed. A new alias, a corrected date of birth, an added address, or detail a parser improvement newly reads from the feed: none of these appear here. Only parties entering or leaving the lists do.
  • A missing authority. If an authority is missing from the snapshot entirely, its parties would appear as a mass removal. The banner at the top of the page says when that is the case, and it means the data is incomplete, not that thousands were delisted.
  • None of this is a determination. Verify any specific party against the issuing authority. See how the data is built.
Latest rebuild

Added

New designations. Screen these against your existing book — a party added today may have been your counterparty yesterday.

Removed

Present in the previous snapshot, absent from this one — typically a delisting, occasionally a reissued id. A removal is not self-executing: confirm the delisting with the issuing authority before releasing anything.

History

Every rebuild's additions and removals, newest first — so the date a party came off a list is still here after the next rebuild.
